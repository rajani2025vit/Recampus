How ReCampus works

A simple space for VIT Chennai students to help each other and reuse what is already on campus.

Report

Post a lost or found item with where and when it happened, so the right person can spot it.

Browse

Search items other students are willing to lend, filtered by category and availability.

Connect

Request an item, get approved by the lender and arrange a hand-over on campus.

Operating Systems project. ReCampus also simulates OS ideas at application level: Lost reports are scheduled like processes and lend items are allocated like exclusive resources. See the OS Scheduler dashboard.
